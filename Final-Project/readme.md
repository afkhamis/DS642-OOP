# Proposal 

The proposal accounts for 10% of the final project grade. Proposal should be limitted to 2 pages. Submit your proposal by the due date indicated on the course sylabus. To begin with you can study an application problem for which parallel computing has been used. Best if you can pick a problem from your own research area. Describe the application, the use of parallelism, and your expectation of its success, strengths/weaknesses, and challenges. Some specific details to consider include the following:

*  A description of the problem in terms of its scietific relevance.

*  Are benchmark results available and if so, how do they compare with the simulations. 

*  Which parallel plateform is used or will be used, e.g. distributed/shared memory, GPU, ....

*  Have you invetigsted how well the application perform compared to the peak performance of the cluser computer you will be using.

*  How does the application "scale" with the number of processors. 
    
*  Can you identify the bottlenecks limiting the performance of the application.

Not all of these details will be available for all applications. But for your final project, you must describe what you find worthwhile regarding the implementation.

# Final Project

Final project should be about understanding performance and the majority of the efforts must be about parallelism; this can involve an existing code, or implementting a method from a research paper. Your final report include the following:

* State the hypothesis of your project. What is the question that you are seeking to explore, understand, and to some extent answer? What might be the results of this investigation?

* Context. Will you be working with others on this? Is it a subproject in the context of a larger research effort? Are variants of it going to be utilized in other classes?

* Key prior work. What are a few (~3) pieces of prior work that this builds upon, is motivated by, to be contrasted with?

* Empirical methodology. What will be done to investigate this question and establish the results? What resources will be needed for that?

* Challenges and Obstacles. What do you anticipate might present a problem that will prevent you from pursuing this investigation as fully as you would like?

You are required to submit a presentation of your final project, which is due by the final exam date. Project final report length should be limited to 10 pages, single-column standard latex. The final project and presentation account for 25% of the the grade. The proposal accounts for 10% of final project grade.
